\textbf{Highlights}

\begin{itemize}
\item The joint speed and density design is unique at every carbon price
\item Given density, carbon favors speed if saved stock emits more per dollar than freight
\item Transit financing raises the cost-optimal speed and leaves the carbon-optimal one
\item Given speed, carbon adds DCs if delivery emits more per dollar than a new DC
\item A small carbon price can favor consolidation even when the carbon minimum is denser
\end{itemize}
